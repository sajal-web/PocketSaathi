🔐 Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 18, 2026

1. Overview

Pocket Saathi is a personal expense tracking app built with a privacy-first philosophy. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data. All information you enter into the app stays entirely on your device.

2. Data We Collect

We collect absolutely nothing. Pocket Saathi does not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Financial data or transaction history
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Any other form of user data

3. Data Storage

All data you enter — including expenses, income, and budget settings — is stored locally on your device using an on-device database (Room/SQLite). This data never leaves your device. We have no servers, no cloud sync, and no backend infrastructure that receives your data.

4. Third-Party Services

Pocket Saathi does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. There are no SDKs in the app that phone home to external servers.

5. Internet Permission

Pocket Saathi does not request internet permission. The app is fully offline and functions without any network connection.

6. Children's Privacy

Pocket Saathi does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected at all, the app is safe for users of all ages.

7. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data via your device's Settings → Apps → Pocket Saathi → Clear Data.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and publish the new version on the app listing page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at:
